### Increment 1: tasks 3 and 4 built in the template

Task 3 (`framework_config`, the schemas and loader for `framework.toml`, `project.json` and `answers.json`) and task 4 (`init new` and the payload manifest) were built test-first in `ai-first-template`, each reviewed by a fresh-eyes pass and by Copilot, and merged as its PRs #1 and #2 (99 tests). What the reviews found is the lesson: the author's tests passed while the TOML emitter wrote invalid TOML for any emoji, a duplicate-key check was quadratic, a directory at `framework.lock` looked like an absent lock, and `pyproject.toml` was hash-locked so a project's first `uv add` would have made every later run refuse. Mutation checks (break a rule, require a test to fail) caught two dead rules and one untested rule that a coverage number would not have. The details are in the spec's *What the build found*.
