### Owner: "is ispc on the eval list?"; then the link to K0lb3's `ispc_texcomp_py`

It was, with a wrong note: I had it down as a C library needing a wrapper of ours. The owner found the
binding: MIT, on PyPI as `ispc_texcomp`, `compress_blocks_bc7/bc5/bc4` over an `RGBASurface` returning the
blocks as bytes, BC7 profiles from `ultrafast` to `slow`, and it resolves for this environment (1.0.1). That
is the owner's BATS bar met on paper: a Python module, no subprocess. A two-minute probe with `uv run --with`
on a synthetic 2K map: BC7 basic 4.4 s and fast 1.8 s per 2048x2048 on one core, BC5 0.01 s, BC4 under 0.01 s, byte-identical on a second run. The spike row names it the leading candidate; the spec's seam gains the
block-bytes shape it returns and a block-compressed DDS writer. The spike still measures quality against
`texconv` and the Linux wheel before it is the default.
→ board (the spike row corrected); the spec's question 2
