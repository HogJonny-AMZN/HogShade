### Owner: "I want to make sure we can cook high quality 32-bit data and 16-bit half EXRs to a respectable format as well, thinking heightmaps here ... do we already have _H?"

We do: `_H` has been in the suffix table since T1 (height, raw, 16-bit authoring, "R16 when the cook says").
What it lacked was the HDR half, and T2's spec now says it: a 16-bit PNG cooks to `R16_UNORM` with every
step kept, a half EXR to `R16_FLOAT`, a float EXR to `R32_FLOAT`, exact in each case; an 8-bit source is the

wasted. The EXR read is the IBL reader extended to a single channel, so no new dependency. Whether the Maya
shell and the wgpu host sample the float formats is T3's host half; `R16_UNORM` is the fallback a host is
sure to read.
